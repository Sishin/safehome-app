KidsHome Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · App: KidsHome: Kids Screen Time (com.agentcodeapps.kidshome)
Developer: AgentCodeApps · Contact: developer.agent47@gmail.com

In short: KidsHome has no account and no servers of its own. Your child's apps, points, history and favourite contacts stay on the phone. The free version shows family-safe ads only inside parent settings, provided by Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases.

What stays on the phone

DataWhy
Approved apps, daily allowance, points and up to 7 days of historyTo run the screen-time rules
Children's first names (or nicknames)To tell profiles apart
Favourite contacts' names and phone numbers, chosen by the parentOne-tap calling. KidsHome reads only the contact a parent picks; it can't see the rest of the address book.
Which app is on screen (Usage access)To count time only while an approved app is open
The parent PIN, stored as a salted hashTo protect parent settings

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It's removed when you uninstall KidsHome or clear its data.

Ads (free version)

Ads appear only in parent settings, behind the PIN, never on the child's screens. They are provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, limited to G-rated content, and never personalised. KidsHome does not use the Android advertising ID.

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address (approximate location), ad interactions, diagnostics and an app-set identifier. Google's ads libraries include code that can handle an email address; KidsHome never asks for, reads or sends one, but because that code ships inside the app it is listed in our Google Play data safety section. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown to the parent.

Children's privacy

KidsHome is designed for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no sharing and no web browsing of its own. Ad partners are configured for child-directed treatment as described above, in line with COPPA and the Google Play Families policy.
