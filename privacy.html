Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · SafeHome – Senior Launcher for Android (com.agentcodeapps.safehome)

In short: SafeHome does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no internet access. Everything stays on the phone.

SafeHome is a simple home screen (launcher) for older adults, set up by a family member. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. No accounts, no servers, no internet

SafeHome has no user accounts and no servers. The app does not request Android's Internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.

2. Information stored on the phone

The family member who sets up the phone enters the information below. It is stored only in the app's private storage on that phone. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Android backup is turned off for SafeHome, so this information is not copied to cloud backups.

InformationWhy
Trusted contacts: name, phone number, relationship, optional photoTo show call buttons on the home screen
Emergency (SOS) contactsTo call family in an emergency
Approved apps and display settingsTo show only the chosen apps, in the chosen size and style
Family PIN and recovery code, stored only as salted, hardware-protected hashesTo protect settings from accidental changes
Daily step counts (optional)To show today's steps and a 7-day chart
Recently screened calls: phone number and time (optional)So the family can review calls that were silenced or blocked

3. Permissions and how they are used

PermissionUse
Make phone callsStart a call when a contact button or SOS is pressed and confirmed.
Call screening role (optional)Silence or block calls from unknown numbers. Android shares only the caller's number with the app, never call audio.
Notification access (optional)Hide notifications from apps the family has not approved, and hide one-time passwords (OTPs) until the user chooses to see them. Notifications are checked on the phone and are never stored or sent anywhere.
Physical activity (optional)Read the phone's built-in step counter to show daily steps.
Default home app roleShow SafeHome when the Home button is pressed.

Each optional permission is requested only when the family member turns on the feature that needs it, with an explanation. SafeHome does not read SMS messages, call logs or the full contact list. When a contact is added, Android's contact picker shares only the chosen contact.

4. Sharing

SafeHome does not share data with anyone. Calls are placed by the phone's own phone app to the numbers the family entered.

5. Children

SafeHome is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Your choices

You can change or delete any information in the app's Admin Mode. You can turn off any permission in Android Settings. You can remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
